# Music credits

Background music is optional in CastLock (assemble.py ducks it under the voice). Any track used must be redistributable and credited. The reference pipeline uses Kevin MacLeod tracks under CC BY 4.0, which require attribution.

Paste-ready credit line for descriptions:

Music: "Reign Supreme" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

When a different track is used, replace the title and keep the licence line that matches the track's actual licence. A track with no clear licence does not get used.
