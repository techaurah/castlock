- This exact photograph is the canonical Tara. Every character shot starts from this still (image-to-video) or composites this still. Nothing else may stand in for her face.
- The still is a real approved source photograph. It must never be replaced by a model-generated image, an img2img derivative, or a LoRA output.
- If a needed shot cannot be made from this still, the shot does not get made from a substitute face. It becomes a text-only or scene-only shot instead.
- Any new still added to this folder must be visually verified against this photograph (same person, same features) and the addition recorded in `../notes.md` with the date and the reason.
